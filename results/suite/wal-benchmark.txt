# WAL durability benchmark

Each WriteAheadLog::append_assignment call writes one 20-byte record and fsyncs it.
Records are not grouped. fflush is not used.
Compiler: Apple LLVM 17.0.0 (clang-1700.6.3.2); C++=201703
Seed: none. Record ids are sequential.
Append warmup: 4 calls on a discarded log, excluded from the timed sample and from replay.
Timed append latency is one append_assignment call: the write plus its fsync.
It does not include dispatcher queueing or matching.
Dispatch latency is submit() through drain() on one worker with a WAL.
It includes queue wait, nearest-driver matching, one record write, and that record's fsync.
Percentile index is floor(p * n). A replay mismatch aborts before this file is written.

Direct appends: 48 timed records.
Sum of timed append calls: 2.254 ms; 21298.234 records/sec.
Append p50/p95/p99/mean ms: 0.043 / 0.063 / 0.180 / 0.047

Dispatcher assignments: 16 drivers, 1 worker, one request at a time.
Sum of submit-to-drain calls: 1.422 ms; 11254.710 assignments/sec.
Dispatch p50/p95/p99/mean ms: 0.078 / 0.211 / 0.211 / 0.089
Individual fsync per record: yes.
